# Figure 8: Teacher margin vs student margin

**Purpose:** Check whether representative students preserve the teacher’s confidence structure at the query level.

![Figure 8: Teacher margin vs student margin](../figures/fig08_teacher_margin_vs_student_margin.png)

## How to read this
Each point is one test query. Better agreement means the student margin rises when the teacher margin rises.

## What it shows
The strongest teacher-margin tracking in the comparison set is s1_hnp_dw100_pw10 (r=0.562).

## Why it matters for the paper
This figure links KD behavior to teacher confidence rather than only final rank metrics.
